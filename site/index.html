Installing the preview

Android

  1. Download RiftBarrage-android.apk on your phone.
  2. Open it and allow installing from your browser when Android asks.
  3. Play in landscape. Your progress stays on the device.

Windows

  1. Download and unzip RiftBarrage-windows-x64.zip.
  2. Run RiftBarrage.exe. Windows SmartScreen may warn about an unrecognized app; choose More info → Run anyway.
  3. Mouse drag, W/S or arrows to fly, Space for the skill, Esc to pause. Gamepads work too.

Check your download against SHA256SUMS.txt on the releases page. This is an early preview: balance will change, and later previews may not keep your progress.
