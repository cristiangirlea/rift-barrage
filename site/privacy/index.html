Privacy

Applies to Rift Barrage for Android and Windows: the preview builds (versions 0.1 to 0.4) downloaded from this site or its GitHub releases, and the builds on Google Play (from version 0.4.1). Last updated 20 November 2026.

What the game collects

The game has no game account, sign-in, advertising, in-app purchases, or developer-configured analytics or crash reporting. Gameplay progress and settings are stored locally on your device.

What stays on your device

Your progress and settings are saved in a file on your own device so you can continue later. Uninstalling the game (Android) or deleting its data folder (Windows) removes it. Settings → Reset profile starts over and keeps the previous file on the device in case you change your mind.

Version 0.3 converts earlier progression into the research system and retains an archive of the original save on the device.

Android permissions

The Android package includes internet permission, which the Unity engine adds. From version 0.4.1 the game turns off Unity's hardware statistics and engine diagnostics, and in our test of version 0.4.1 the game made no network connections. Earlier previews had both Unity features switched on; Unity describes the device and network data they process in its game player privacy policy.

Google Play delivers the game and its download data under Google's own privacy policy.

Future versions

If a later version adds online features, ads or purchases, this page will be updated before that version is offered, and it will explain what is collected and why.
